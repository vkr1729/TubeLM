For EACH source video or article, write a **narrative summary** (2-3 paragraphs) that retells the argument arc as a story. - If the creator uses a powerful analogy or metaphor that makes the idea click, include it.
- Keep it tight. If the entire argument can be retold in 2 paragraphs, do NOT pad to 3.
